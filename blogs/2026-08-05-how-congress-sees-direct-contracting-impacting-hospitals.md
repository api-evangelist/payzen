Congress heard testimony that expanding direct contracting could cut costs, speed provider payments, and reduce clinician burnout, particularly benefiting rural hospitals, but the resulting drop in ED and urgent care visits would also cut hospital revenue, and related bills on facility fees and claims-data sharing drew pushback from hospital advocates over administrative burden.
