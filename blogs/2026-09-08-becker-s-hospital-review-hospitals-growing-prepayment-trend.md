More providers are requiring payment before a medical procedure. The share of U.S. healthcare providers building prepayment into the estimate process — whether by encouraging it, requiring it, or keeping a card on file — climbed to more than 9 in 10 in 2026, up from 81% in 2025, according to survey data from the Healthcare Financial Management Association and PayZen.
